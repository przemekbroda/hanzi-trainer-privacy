# Hanzi Trainer privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Hanzi Trainer stores your data on your device. We have no server for this app and we don't track your progress. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics of how you study.

Hanzi Trainer is developed by Przemysław Broda, who is the data controller. In this policy, "we" means Przemysław Broda. You can reach us at the address under Contact.

## What stays with you

Your decks, characters, mnemonics, notes, example words, reviews, progress and settings are saved on your device. We can't see them and we don't receive them.

- What you write on the screen is checked on your device.
- Reminders are scheduled by your device, without a push service.
- A backup or character file that you export goes where you choose to send it.

## Backups made by your phone

Your phone's own backup can copy the app's data. We have no access to these copies.

- Android can back up app data to your Google account. On Android 9 and later that backup is encrypted with your screen lock. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.
- iPhone and iPad can include app data in iCloud Backup or in a backup on your computer. You can manage this in the device's settings.

## Purchases

Apple or Google takes the payment, under its own privacy policy. We never receive your card details.

We use RevenueCat to check purchases and to unlock what you bought. The app contacts RevenueCat when it starts and when you view products, buy or restore, also if you never buy anything. RevenueCat receives:

- a random ID made for the app, which is not your name, email or an advertising ID;
- your purchase history in this app, with the store's proof of purchase;
- technical details: app version, device brand and model, operating system version, language, the country of your store and, as with any internet connection, your IP address.

We use this to provide and restore purchases, to prevent fraud and to see sales totals. It contains nothing about what you study. We don't sell it or use it for advertising or to track you across other apps and websites.

RevenueCat processes this data for us and has to protect it at least as well as this policy describes. It is in the United States, so the data is processed there. Its own policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## How data is protected

Data on your device is protected by the device's app sandbox and screen lock. Connections to RevenueCat and the stores are encrypted with HTTPS. Files you export are not encrypted, so share them with care.

## How long data is kept

- Data on your device stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
- A deleted deck or character is hidden, but its record can stay in the app's storage and in exported backups until then.
- Backups made by your phone are kept as Google or Apple describe.
- Purchase records are kept while they are needed to provide and restore purchases or as the law requires.
- Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to answer you.

## How to delete your data

- On Android, clear the app's storage in the system settings or uninstall the app.
- On iPhone and iPad, delete the app.
- Delete backups in your Google or Apple account, and any files you exported. Otherwise your data can return when you reinstall.
- To have your purchase record at RevenueCat deleted, email us with the order number from the store's receipt, so we can find it. You may then need to restore your purchase.

## Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, we process purchase data to provide what you bought, and for our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and knowing sales totals. You can ask to see, correct, delete or receive that data, or object to its use. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and its date are published in the app and where this policy is hosted.
